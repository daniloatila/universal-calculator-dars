# Geometry mathematics and bounds

[Português brasileiro](limits.pt-BR.md)

Geometry uses exact rational dimensions and Cartesian coordinates in one common length unit. Enter scalar dimensions or the coordinates of a point separated by spaces. Separate point rows by semicolons or line breaks. Cells accept signed integers, decimals, exponent notation and fractions, as in `1.5`, `2e-3` and `3/4`. Enter the unit separately through the `unit` option. The accepted length units are `u`, `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `km`, `in`, `ft`, `yd` and `mi`. The default `u` represents an unspecified common unit. There is no unit conversion in this stage. [Literal parser](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry/value.rs) · [Operation and unit contract](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry.rs)

| Operation | Input and output |
| --- | --- |
| Rectangle | Positive width and height. Returns area, perimeter and the diagonal. |
| Circle | Positive radius. Returns diameter, circumference and area. |
| Triangle | Three positive side lengths satisfying the strict triangle inequality. Returns the perimeter, Heron area, side classification and angle classification. |
| Polygon | A simple, nonzero-area planar boundary in vertex order. Returns shoelace area, edge-sum perimeter and clockwise or counterclockwise orientation. Convex and concave polygons are supported. |
| Sphere | Positive radius. Returns surface area and volume. |
| Cylinder | Positive radius and perpendicular height of a right circular cylinder. Returns base area, lateral area, total surface area and volume. |
| Cone | Positive radius and perpendicular height of a right circular cone. Returns slant height, base area, lateral area, total surface area and volume. |
| Prism | Simple polygon boundary, a vertical bar and positive perpendicular height. Returns base area, base perimeter, lateral area, total surface area and volume of the right prism. |
| Distance | Two points of the same dimension, either both 2D or both 3D. Returns Euclidean distance. Repeated points give zero. |
| Angle | Three points of the same dimension, either all 2D or all 3D. The middle point is the common vertex and both rays must be nonzero. Returns the undirected principal angle in degrees by default or radians with `angle=rad`. |
| Segments | Four 2D point rows with two distinct endpoints per segment. Two pairs of rows can also be separated by a vertical bar. Returns disjoint, proper crossing, single-point touch or overlap, with exact coordinates where applicable. |

## Domain, exactness and proof

- Side and angular classification use equality of rational side lengths and comparison of the squared longest side with the sum of the other squared sides. Heron's radicand uses exact rational arithmetic, so nearly degenerate admissible triangles retain a positive area. [Heron formula](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/HeronsFormula.html) · [Triangle implementation](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry.rs)
- A polygon accepts 3–32 distinct vertices and closes automatically. An optional final repeat of the first vertex is removed. Adjacent edges may meet only at their shared endpoint. All nonadjacent edges must be disjoint. An extra collinear vertex continuing along a side is allowed. Backtracking overlap, repeated interior vertices, nonadjacent touching, self-crossing edges and zero area report a domain error. [Simple polygon definition](https://doc.cgal.org/5.2/Polygon/group__PkgPolygon2Functions.html) · [Shoelace formula](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/ShoelaceFormula.html) · [Exact boundary tests](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry/cartesian.rs)
- Segment orientation uses the sign of the rational determinant of two displacements from a common point. Opposite signs on both pairs identify a proper crossing. Exact collinearity and closed-segment order distinguish endpoint touches and interval overlap. A proper crossing is constructed with a rational line parameter. An overlap returns its two endpoints ordered by (x), then by (y). Nearly collinear inputs retain exact signs without a floating-point tolerance. [Robust determinant predicates](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html) · [Contact classification](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry/cartesian.rs)
- The angle lies between (0) and (pi) radians, inclusive. The sign and exact squared cosine determine whether the angle is one of the recognized special values. Collinear nonzero rays give (0) or (pi). Other angles retain an exact symbolic `acos` expression, with a factor of `180/pi` for degrees. [Dot product and angle](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-3/pages/2-3-the-dot-product) · [Angle implementation](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry.rs)
- Cylinder, cone and prism use right solids. Base and lateral areas have square units. Volume has cubic units. Total surface area includes the relevant bases. The cone uses slant height for its lateral area and perpendicular height for volume. The prism uses the validated polygon base, its perimeter and the perpendicular height. [Geometric formulas](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/c-geometric-formulas) · [Volume and surface](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/9-6-solve-geometry-applications-volume-and-surface-area)

Results use labeled mathematical quantities such as `area=3; perimeter=7; diagonal=5/2`. Units appear in the record by result name, using the base symbol, `^2` or `^3`, and `deg` or `rad` for the angle. Coordinates on segment contacts use the selected length unit. Pure rational quantities and classifications are exact. A returned `pi`, nonrational square root or inverse cosine is symbolic. Perfect rational squares simplify to fractions or integers. Identical radicands combine automatically. The engine retains other square roots in equivalent exact form. Precision, tolerance and error-bound fields remain empty.

## Resources and validation

The request allows up to 4,096 UTF-8 bytes. Each scalar literal allows 256 bytes, up to 128 mantissa digits in each decimal component and decimal exponent magnitude up to 256. Intermediate rational numerators and denominators have a 4,096-bit bound. The result text has the shared 32,768-byte bound. Incompatible dimensions or invalid geometry report `domain`. Invalid syntax reports `invalid_input` and excess resource use reports `limit`. The supported classes exclude curved polygon edges, holes, oblique solids, directed angles and 3D segment intersections. [Exact arithmetic guards](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/geometry/value.rs) · [Shared result guard](../../../crates/dars-universal/src/lib.rs)

The bounded engine uses the existing arbitrary-precision Rust rational types. Direct exact determinants suit rational inputs and the small polygon cap. The boundary check examines at most 496 edge pairs. A plane sweep or adaptive-predicate library can serve larger or floating-point geometry workloads. SymPy supplies an independent reference for measures and topology, with additional tests for invariance under scale, translation, vertex order and rigid transformations.
