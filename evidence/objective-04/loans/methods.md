# Loans methods

Português brasileiro · [Métodos de empréstimos](methods.pt-BR.md)

The schedule uses monthly civil dates from an explicit disbursement date. With no first due date, the following month starts the schedule. Later dates retain the original due-day anchor and clamp to the last day of short months. A row accrues exactly one contractual period at the specified nonnegative fractional rate. Stepped rate changes take effect on the specified row and remain active until the next change.

- Price solves the remaining fixed installment `B*i/[1-(1+i)^(-n)]`, including `B/n` at zero rate. SAC fixes scheduled principal amortization at `B/n` on initialization or recast. Both accrue interest as opening balance times the period rate. [Banco Central do Brasil fixed installments](https://www3.bcb.gov.br/CALCIDADAO/publico/exibirMetodologiaFinanciamentoPrestacoesFixas.do?method=exibirMetodologiaFinanciamentoPrestacoesFixas) · [CAIXA SAC schedule methodology](https://www.caixa.gov.br/Downloads/habitacao-documentos-gerais/passos_indexadores_amortizacao.pdf)
- Uniform payments use the supplied fixed amount and adjust the closing payment to settle any remainder. A fixed payment that fails to cover due interest during amortization produces a domain error. Bullet pays interest periodically and repays principal at maturity. Single payment capitalizes interest and settles principal and interest at maturity, with any elective extra principal or fees treated as separate events.
- Grace periods either capitalize interest or require an interest-only payment. Extra principal follows contractual principal, and its amount is capped at the remaining balance. Early termination ends subsequent charges. Under `reduce_term`, the scheduled amount continues until settlement, subject to Price rate repricing and closing adjustment. Under `reduce_payment`, an extra payment recasts Price and SAC over the remaining contractual term. A requested payoff collects open principal, that period's interest and any due fee. [CAIXA contract and early amortization FAQ](https://www.caixa.gov.br/voce/habitacao/perguntas-frequentes-contrato/Paginas/default.aspx)
- Fee events are separate cash flows. The upfront fee is paid at disbursement and belongs in the total paid. Proposal comparison ranks exact nominal cost, defined as total paid minus financed principal, with tied proposals retained. Nominal cost depends on scale and duration.

In `per_row` mode the ledger starts with principal, fees, extras and any uniform payment at currency scale and rounds accrual and scheduled amortization on each row. The closing row reconciles the remaining principal. In `final` mode internal arithmetic is rational and each presentation rounds independently, so rounded rows can differ from displayed totals by minor units. For each exact or per-row ledger, `closing=opening+capitalized-amortization-extra` and `payment=interest-capitalized+amortization+extra+fees`. The schedule supports up to 600 installment periods with at most 40 rows per page.

Economic effective cost solves for the annual rate making the projected cash flows match the initial net disbursement. It discounts by Actual/365 Fixed and includes supplied charges on their scheduled dates. It is an economic estimate under the chosen contract model. Regulatory Brazilian CET has its own applicable scope, calculation and disclosure rules under CMN Resolution 4,881, effective 2021-02-01, and is outside this operation. [CMN Resolution 4,881](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/especialnor/Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o4881.pdf)
