# Regression and compatibility scope

[Português brasileiro](regression.pt-BR.md)

The original ten workspaces retain their request and Calculation Proof schemas. Schema-one records from earlier versions remain readable. The commerce workspaces add module identifiers and bounded request operations. The underlying worker scheduler, five profiles, preference settings and offline controls are shared with the previous workspaces.

The Objective 03 audit mapped 602 earlier checks, 2,355 advanced independent cases, 305 advanced CLI/WASM comparisons, 420 input-notation comparisons, 146 Chrome tests and 17 Windows checks. The 0.4.0 audit records the corresponding suite coverage in its test ledger. Counts have overlapping scopes and describe the individual verification channels. [Objective 03 baseline](../objective-03/revision-01/tests.json)

Finance testing includes a 42-transition matrix over five workspaces, with 210 comparisons of complete before-and-after Calculation Proof records. Dated flows keep the amount and its date in one editable row. Mixed-source inputs retain their original decimals across language and preference changes. The existing ten-workspace matrix runs alongside the finance checks.
