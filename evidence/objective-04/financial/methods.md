# Financial methods

Português brasileiro · [Métodos financeiros](methods.pt-BR.md)

The engine accepts fractional rates and decimal amounts as strings. An input rate of `0.02` means 2%. Monetary results use the selected ISO currency scale and rounding policy. Rational results include exact values alongside the formatted displays. Integer powers and ordinary arithmetic use `BigRational` with bounded operands. Transcendental operations use the pinned `astro-float-num` 0.3.6 at 320 working bits. Working precision and the output's 16 decimal places are independent of a certified error bound. [ISO 4217 maintenance agency](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/market-reference-data/data-standards.html) · [astro-float-num 0.3.6](https://docs.rs/astro-float-num/0.3.6/astro_float_num/)

Simple interest applies the factor `1+i*n`. Compound interest and lump-sum value use `(1+i)^n`. Discount offers commercial simple `FV*(1-i*n)`, rational simple `FV/(1+i*n)` and rational compound `FV/(1+i)^n`. Annuities use a geometric sum, with the first payment at `t=0` in advance and `t=1` in arrears. The zero-rate limit is `n` payments. Nominal and effective rate conversions use the stated compounding frequency. Rate and period inversions solve the lump-sum equation. [NIST Handbook 135, 2025 edition](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/hb/2025/NIST.HB.135e2025.pdf)

Periodic amounts start at index 0. Dated amounts are sorted, measured from the earliest supplied date and summed when dates or day-count positions coincide. The year fraction uses Actual/365 Fixed by default, Actual/360 or European 30E/360. For 30E/360, both day-of-month values are capped at 30. Rates are annual for dated series and per period for periodic series. [ISDA day-count conventions](https://www.isda.org/2008/12/22/30-360-day-count-conventions/)

- NPV sums all discounted signed amounts. MIRR compounds positive amounts to the horizon at the reinvestment rate and discounts negative amounts to the origin at the financing rate.
- Payback finds the first nonnegative cumulative balance after an initial outlay. It linearly interpolates in the crossing interval. Discounted payback applies the same rule to discounted amounts. The profitability index divides the present value of positive flows by the magnitude of the present value of negative flows. [Microsoft MIRR reference](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/functions/mirr-function)
- Periodic IRR maps `i > -1` to positive `x=1/(1+i)` and finds the positive real roots of `Σ C_t*x^t`. Linear and rational quadratic cases are solved directly. The general path uses square-free factors, exact Sturm counts and rational isolating intervals, with multiplicity preserved. A periodic interval is at most `1e-12` wide in rate space. The known `[-100, 230, -132]` pair yields exact rates `1/10` and `1/5`. [NIST DLMF polynomial zeros](https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.11) · [SymPy polynomial isolation reference](https://docs.sympy.org/latest/modules/polys/reference.html)
- With one chronological sign change, the equivalent dated discount polynomial has one positive root. The implementation estimates that root in `[-0.999999999999, 10000]` using high-precision signs and a bracket width of at most `1e-12`. The numeric signs do not constitute certification. Dated series with multiple sign changes return `completeness_uncertified`. Single-sign series return `none`; the all-zero series returns `indeterminate`.

The calculation caps periods at 600, cash-flow rows at 128, dated span at 36,600 calendar days, and periodic IRR degree at 48 after removing zero coefficients at the ends. Periodic isolation caps the number of intervals processed and the size of rational operands. The proof distinguishes an empty certified solution set from an unresolved or uncertified search.

RPN was evaluated as an optional input mode. It would need a dedicated keystroke and stack contract, accessible stack state, and proof replay across languages. The structured inputs cover the required calculation paths, so this objective defers RPN.

Run the independent standard-library Python oracle against the built CLI with `python -X utf8 tests/verify-financial-loans.py target/release/dars.exe`. It uses exact fractions for elementary operations and polynomial root checks, 80-digit decimal arithmetic for dated calculations, and stable error fixtures.
