# Brazilian tax rules in DARS 0.4.0

[Português brasileiro](rules.pt-BR.md)

Dataset `BR-2026.1` was reviewed on 2026-11-25. The application evaluates tax events dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31, inclusive. The annual mode calculates calendar year 2026 for assessment year 2027. In the investment modes the event date is the redemption settlement date, so a purchase may precede 2026. The listed dates define the coverage of this dataset. Several underlying laws have a broader validity period.

The calculator runs locally and accepts BRL amounts in whole cents. Proof records include the rule ID, jurisdiction, snapshot version, supported period, event date, review date, rounding policy, formula and official sources. The scope of each operation assumes the facts in the table below are applicable to the transaction. The engine rejects other dates with `unsupported`, corrupted field structures with `invalid_input` and impossible or non-cent amounts with `domain`.

## Rounding and exclusions

- The eSocial INSS calculation truncates each marginal band. The other simulations round named money amounts to cents, nearest with ties away from zero. The income-tax calculation rounds progressive tax and relief individually. Annual simplified deduction is rounded at cents before the base calculation. The annual statutory formula yields BRL 0.02 of relief at exactly BRL 88,200, and the rule excludes higher gross amounts from relief.
- The investment tax simulation rounds the IOF charged to cents before subtracting it from realized gain. IR is then rounded separately. The engine covers 1–36,600 elapsed calendar days. For LCI or LCA, it accepts only the non-price-indexed case purchased at issue from 2025-05-26 with a holding of at least six calendar months. The calculation uses the actual redemption and assumes compliance with any longer contractual lockup. Price-indexed issues, coupons, fees, partial redemptions, secondary trades, loss carryovers and other asset or taxpayer types require other rules. [CMN Resolution 5.215/2025](https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/exibenormativo?numero=5215&tipo=RESOLU%C3%87%C3%83O+CMN)
- Payroll excludes combined income sources, 13th salary, vacation, PLR, special social security regimes, employer charges, FGTS and other cash deductions. Annual IRPF excludes high-income minimum tax, credits other than the given withholding, special categories of income and return preparation. The dependent count accepted by the interface is bounded at 30.
- IOF on credit, foreign exchange, insurance, funds with special treatment and same-day redemptions has separate rules. ICMS excludes substitution, interstate rates, FCP, credits, Simples Nacional, reduced rates, exemptions and other taxes. The ICMS and IBS/CBS examples use distinct bases and can be run independently.
- Article 348 provides for compensation when IBS or CBS is paid in 2026 and preserves ordinary PIS/Cofins payment for taxpayers granted dispensation. The calculator models the nominal IBS and CBS amounts and the conditional payment waiver. It leaves offsets, actual collection in unconfirmed cases, separate PIS/Cofins liability and special regimes outside the estimate. [LC 214/2025, art. 348](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp214compilado.htm#art348)

## IBS/CBS after the supported year

The statute prescribes state and municipal IBS of 0.05% each in 2027–2028, and provides for a 0.1 percentage-point reduction in the applicable ordinary CBS rate in those years, subject to statutory exceptions. The CBS reference requires a Senate resolution, and the TCU's 11.3% reference calculation was still a proposal in the official sources consulted on 2026-11-25. The ADCT provides for the ordinary ICMS and ISS rates to be reduced to 90%, 80%, 70% and 60% of their applicable rates in 2029–2032, respectively, as IBS replaces them. DARS rejects calculation for these later years with the present set of rules. [LC 214/2025, arts. 344, 347 and 349](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp214compilado.htm#art344), [EC 132/2023, ADCT art. 128](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/emendas/emc/emc132.htm), [TCU submission to the Senate](https://portal.tcu.gov.br/imprensa/noticias/presidente-do-tcu-entrega-ao-senado-acordao-sobre-aliquota-de-referencia-da-cbs)
